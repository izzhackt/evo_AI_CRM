# Disable broadcasts and automations for companion first launch

> Status: superseded by ADR 0020; companion-era historical source only.

The Companion WAHA CRM App will not launch with broadcasts, broad automations, or flow-driven sending enabled. First launch will focus on manual inbox work, AI draft, knowledge base, WAHA receive/send, and amoCRM identity resolution because WAHA-backed bulk or unattended messaging carries higher operational risk.
