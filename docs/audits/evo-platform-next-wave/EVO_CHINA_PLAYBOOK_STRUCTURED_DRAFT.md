## 1. Role and scope

### Stable operational intent extracted from the DOCX

The China Curator:

- accompanies the student from consultation/handoff through admission, visa preparation, departure, arrival and ongoing support;
- helps compare universities/programs against the student's goal, preparation, language and budget;
- issues a case-specific document list, checks documents and tracks corrections;
- coordinates university/partner communications and records provider requests;
- monitors application and visa-case progress and keeps the student informed;
- records each student's stage, next action and deadline in the company system;
- protects personal data and keeps management reporting current;
- maintains current China knowledge and reports process problems.

### Platform correction

- OZO is a business function, not a separate technical role.
- In Platform v1 the assigned `curator` owns applications, documents, visa, tasks and post-handoff communications for the case.
- Finance/Admin alone confirms payment or refund evidence.
- A Curator or AI observation is not a final university, document, visa or financial decision.
- Admission, scholarship and visa are external outcomes and must not be guaranteed.

## 2. Entry conditions after OP to OZO handoff

Before Stage 1, the Platform case should contain or explicitly mark missing:

- confirmed contract/handoff evidence;
- Admin-assigned Curator;
- applicant and decision-participant roles;
- education level and academic summary;
- intended country/region, program direction and intake assumption;
- language evidence/summary;
- budget band;
- approved commercial fields/promises;
- unresolved questions;
- next action, owner and due date;
- consent and communication language;
- finance status/stop factor without private transaction details.

The DOCX starts with consultation but does not define the OP-to-OZO authority transition, contract evidence, Admin assignment or finance boundary. Those must come from Platform, not be inferred from chat.

## 3. Structured 12-stage lifecycle

### Stage 1 — Consultation and route definition

From the employee draft:

- clarify education level: language course, bachelor, master, doctoral study, internship or another supported route;
- clarify program direction, preferred region and budget;
- assess reported Chinese/English level and available evidence;
- prepare a preliminary shortlist.

Platform output:

- reviewed admissions-route assumptions;
- missing facts and evidence;
- shortlist task with owner/due date.

Not yet verified:

- the DOCX suggests a shortlist of 3–5 universities. Treat the number as an EVO operating preference requiring owner approval, not a country rule.

### Stage 2 — Case-specific document checklist

The DOCX lists passport, education/status evidence, transcript, Study Plan/Personal Statement, recommendations, language certificate when required, photo, medical certificate, criminal-record certificate for long programs and financial evidence when required.

Approved EVO internal material also lists application form, passport, education evidence with English notarized translation, medical certificate, criminal-record certificate with English notarized translation, electronic photo, English motivation/recommendations and an English self-introduction video.

Required managed rule:

- these are preliminary categories only;
- final checklist depends on university, level, program, scholarship and visa route;
- every document becomes a versioned Platform requirement slot;
- file presence does not equal approval;
- exact format, validity, language, certification and medical form require current case-specific evidence.

### Stage 3 — University and program shortlist

Compare:

- approved institution/program identity;
- teaching language;
- intake and deadline;
- tuition/application fee/scholarship;
- program duration;
- accommodation;
- entry requirements;
- source freshness and availability.

Evidence boundary:

- the approved 2026 EVO working table can support preliminary consultation;
- before payment or final submission, the Curator must reconfirm availability, deadline and final amount with the university or authorized partner;
- accreditation/ranking/license claims in the DOCX require current authoritative evidence and must not be inferred from a marketing table.

### Stage 4 — Translation, notarization and legalization

Create one action per document:

- target language;
- translation requirement;
- notarization requirement;
- apostille or consular legalization requirement, if any;
- source and case condition;
- responsible person, due date and evidence.

The DOCX gives general examples but no jurisdiction/applicability matrix. Therefore no universal China legalization rule is approved by this draft.

### Stage 5 — Application preparation and submission

Curator work:

- prepare the university portal/application form;
- attach the reviewed case-specific package;
- obtain staff confirmation before submission;
- record provider acknowledgment, submission evidence and any application fee separately;
- track requests for additional information.

Assistant boundary:

- may find missing items and draft a submission checklist;
- may not submit or say that submission occurred without Platform evidence.

### Stage 6 — Preliminary or conditional response

The DOCX calls this `Pre-admission` and warns that it is not the final visa document.

Required handling:

- store the received provider document privately;
- Curator verifies applicant, university, program, intake, conditions and authenticity/source;
- record outstanding conditions and next action;
- message template must describe the exact document, not generically promise final admission.

Mutable claim requiring verification:

- `2–4 weeks`, or up to `6 weeks`, is an unsourced general estimate in the DOCX. It cannot be presented as a current promise.

### Stage 7 — Final admission evidence

The DOCX refers to Letter of Admission and JW201/JW202.

Required handling:

- Curator verifies names, institution, program, dates and conditions;
- record exact received document type and source;
- do not infer visa eligibility or approval from admission evidence;
- verify which forms are currently issued/required for the exact route.

Mutable claims requiring verification:

- the DOCX's JW201/JW202 role descriptions;
- a `2–6 week` post-pre-admission estimate;
- any link between tuition payment and issue timing.

### Stage 8 — University/provider invoice and payment evidence

Required separation:

- university tuition;
- accommodation;
- application/deposit fee;
- EVO service fee;
- other third-party costs.

Curator may explain a verified university invoice and request that Finance/Admin review evidence. Curator and assistant do not mark payment confirmed and do not provide bank details unless they are taken from current verified invoice/workflow evidence.

### Stage 9 — Visa preparation and provider decision

The DOCX mentions X1/X2 routes and a passport, admission evidence, JW form, visa form/photo, medical certificate, payment/accommodation and flight evidence.

Platform treatment:

- statuses remain `not_required`, `not_started`, `docs`, `appointment`, `submitted`, `approved`, `rejected`, `closed`;
- the exact checklist is instantiated from current official evidence for the applicant's citizenship/residency and place of application;
- appointment, submission and decision require separate evidence;
- a visa decision is never inferred from readiness or university admission.

All categories, duration thresholds, forms, appointment route and document requirements from the DOCX are `verification_required` until checked against current official Chinese visa/consular sources for the actual applicant jurisdiction.

### Stage 10 — Pre-departure preparation

Proposed checklist categories:

- verified passport/visa validity;
- current entry rule;
- university arrival instruction;
- verified housing;
- travel booking;
- insurance;
- copies and safe storage of documents;
- airport/contact plan;
- first-days communication plan;
- university-specific packing and prohibited-item guidance.

Every time-sensitive item is rechecked immediately before travel.

### Stage 11 — Arrival and local formalities

The DOCX includes local accommodation registration, medical examination, residence formalities, student ID/campus registration and bank setup.

Required evidence model:

- each task has owner, due date source, status and completion evidence;
- university-managed and student-managed tasks are distinct;
- current government/university instructions override the draft;
- missed deadlines or blocked work remain visible to the Curator.

Mutable claims requiring official verification include the `24-hour` accommodation-registration statement, the `30-day` residence-permit statement, medical-exam applicability and required documents.

### Stage 12 — Adaptation and ongoing support

The DOCX suggests periodic contact, study/accommodation help and visa/residence renewal reminders.

Before approval, EVO must define:

- contracted support duration and service boundary;
- renewal ownership and reminder source;
- after-arrival communication cadence;
- which campus/life issues are informational versus case work;
- closure criteria and ongoing owner.

## 5. Staff-reviewed message templates

These templates preserve the useful intent of the DOCX while removing unsourced promises. Every template has `requires_staff_review=true` and `send_authority=false`.

### `handoff.introduction`

```text
Здравствуйте, [student_display_name]. Меня зовут [curator_name], я назначен(а) вашим куратором EVO по направлению Китай. Я вижу подтверждённый маршрут: [route_summary]. Сейчас следующий шаг — [next_action]. Перед началом я сверю ваши данные и подготовлю план с актуальными требованиями для выбранного маршрута.
```

### `documents.initial_checklist`

```text
[student_display_name], подготовлен предварительный список документов для [institution/program/intake]. Пожалуйста, ориентируйтесь на приложенный чек-лист версии [checklist_version]. Требования могут отличаться по университету, программе и стипендии, поэтому я проверю каждый файл до подачи. Сейчас нужны: [verified_requirements].
```

### `documents.correction_required`

```text
По документу [document_title] нужна корректировка: [review_reason]. Основание: [requirement_citation]. Пожалуйста, загрузите новую версию до [verified_due_date_or_no_date]. Предыдущая версия сохранится в истории.
```

### `shortlist.ready_for_review`

```text
Я подготовил(а) варианты по вашим критериям [criteria]. По каждому варианту указаны только подтверждённые на [checked_at] сведения и вопросы, которые ещё нужно уточнить. Выберите интересующие варианты, после чего мы отдельно проверим наличие мест, дедлайн и финальную стоимость перед подачей или оплатой.
```

### `application.submitted`

```text
Заявка в [institution] по программе [program] зафиксирована как отправленная [submitted_at]. Подтверждение подачи: [safe_evidence_ref]. Следующий шаг — [next_action]. Срок ответа зависит от университета; я сообщу, когда появится подтверждённое обновление.
```

This template is allowed only after submission evidence already exists.

### `pre_admission.received`

```text
Получен документ [document_title] от [institution]. Он означает: [verified_meaning]. Это [is_or_is_not_final_decision]. Остались условия: [outstanding_conditions]. Следующий подтверждённый шаг — [next_action].
```

### `admission.received`

```text
Получено подтверждение от [institution] по [program/intake]. Я проверил(а) основные данные: [verified_fields]. Следующий этап — [next_action]. Это решение университета не является решением по визе.
```

### `invoice.received`

```text
Получен счёт от [provider] на [verified_amount_and_currency] со сроком [verified_due_date]. Назначение платежа: [invoice_purpose]. Перед оплатой проверьте реквизиты в самом подтверждённом счёте. Статус оплаты в Platform подтверждает Finance/Admin после получения evidence.
```

### `visa.checklist_ready`

```text
Подготовлен визовый чек-лист для вашего маршрута [route_and_jurisdiction], проверенный по источнику [source] на [checked_at]. Сейчас нужны: [requirements]. Подача и решение будут зафиксированы отдельно; получение визы заранее не гарантируется.
```

### `predeparture.checklist`

```text
Перед выездом сверим актуальный чек-лист на [verification_date]: [verified_items]. Пожалуйста, не используйте старую памятку как единственный источник — правила въезда и требования университета перепроверяются непосредственно перед поездкой.
```

### `arrival.first_days`

```text
После прибытия выполните задачи из вашего актуального плана: [verified_first_days_tasks]. Для каждой задачи указаны срок, ответственный и источник. Если университет уже выполнил задачу, загрузите или сообщите подтверждение — куратор отметит её после проверки.
```

### `support.periodic_checkin`

```text
Здравствуйте, [student_display_name]. Проверяю текущий этап: [current_stage]. Открытые действия: [open_actions]. Ближайшая подтверждённая дата: [verified_due_date_or_none]. Сообщите, появились ли новые документы или вопросы по учебному/миграционному статусу.
```

## 8. Gaps in the employee DOCX before managed approval

1. No signed approval, version, effective date, owner or review cadence.
2. No authoritative source register or checked dates.
3. No applicant-jurisdiction/applicability matrix.
4. Generic document lists are not linked to specific university/program/scholarship/visa requirements.
5. Deadlines, time estimates, money thresholds and immigration rules are unsourced.
6. Message templates can imply completed actions without requiring Platform evidence.
7. No typed Platform status/evidence mapping or independent workstream statuses.
8. Finance authority is not separated from Curator responsibility.
9. Sensitive-document handling, retention and private Storage rules are absent.
10. No rule preserves the country-playbook version already applied to an existing case.
11. No explicit AI boundary; the draft assumes a human Curator.
12. Ongoing-support duration and case closure criteria are undefined.
