# EVO Country Coverage Audit

Status: read-only evidence audit; no knowledge publication, production import or provider claim.

Audit date: 2026-08-17.

## Question answered

This audit distinguishes four things that are easy to confuse:

1. **Operational playbook** — how the Curator actually runs a case from handoff through arrival/support.
2. **Country facts** — programs, costs, documents, visa rules or marketing information.
3. **Curator regulation gap** — missing roles, stage actions, evidence, message templates and Platform mapping.
4. **Staff material awaited** — information that needs a country employee to supply or approve it before managed publication.

Having many country facts does not mean EVO has a usable Curator playbook. A playbook can also exist as an employee draft without being current, sourced or approved for AI.

## Classification scale

Operational playbook:

- `strong draft` — covers most curator stages, but is not yet managed/approved/current;
- `substantial fragments` — enough operational material exists, but it is split across notes and not one approved versioned playbook;
- `partial` — one or more process notes exist, but full case operation is missing;
- `facts only` — country knowledge exists without a Curator operating procedure;
- `missing` — no usable country-specific operation was found in the approved layer.

Staff material:

- `received` — a country employee document exists;
- `partial` — operational evidence exists but needs consolidation/owner approval;
- `awaited` — ask the country employee to prepare the China-template package.

## Country findings

### China

Evidence found:

- 11-page staff DOCX with job responsibilities, 12 admissions stages, 12 stage-message templates and entry/adaptation guidance;
- approved internal China document categories;
- owner-approved guidance that the final document list is university/program/scholarship-specific;
- a large 2026 EVO program table for preliminary consultation;
- managed-review candidate concerning university and visa document distinction.

Strength:

- best single-document structure for creating the common country template;
- covers handoff-style introduction through ongoing support;
- includes practical student communication.

Gaps:

- document is unsigned/undated/unversioned and outside the approved vault;
- application windows, processing times, visa forms/categories, immigration/customs thresholds and arrival deadlines lack current official citations;
- generic lists are not bound to typed case requirements;
- message templates can claim submission, payment or approval without Platform evidence;
- no separate Finance authority, privacy boundary or applied-version retention.

Classification: `strong draft`, not production-ready.

2. Verify every mutable fact from current official/university sources.
3. Approve one immutable managed version.

## Common gaps across countries

1. Country facts and Curator operating procedure are stored as different fragments.
2. Most countries lack one immutable playbook version pinned to a case.
3. Mutable facts often lack `checked_at`, `valid_until` and verification trigger.
4. Generic document lists are not linked to university/program/scholarship/visa conditions.
5. Message templates are rarely tied to Platform evidence before claiming completion.
6. Independent application/document/visa/finance/housing/insurance/travel states are not consistently expressed.
7. Country employee ownership and review cadence are missing.
8. No reviewed country playbook is yet proven in production retrieval for OZO Assistant.

## Recommended employee message

Send the China DOCX plus this instruction to every active country owner:

> Пожалуйста, подготовьте регламент куратора по вашей стране примерно в структуре китайского примера: роль и границы ответственности, путь студента от передачи из продаж до приезда/адаптации, документы и как их проверять, подача/виза/статусы и evidence, шаблоны сообщений по этапам. Отдельно отметьте всё, что меняется: программы, дедлайны, цены, визовые и въездные правила — для каждого укажите текущий официальный источник и дату проверки. Не включайте данные студентов, пароли или переписки. Если чего-то нет или вы не уверены, так и отметьте — не придумывайте.
