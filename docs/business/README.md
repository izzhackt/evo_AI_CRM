## Remaining Authoritative Inputs

The following items must be supplied and approved by a named EVO owner before
they can become operational truth:

- services, packages, prices, currencies, discounts, and payment terms outside
  the approved China and Malaysia content;
- the complete active-country, university, partner, intake, and deadline matrix;
- scholarship/grant policy and evidence requirements;
- response-time commitments and escalation contacts;
- approved WhatsApp templates in Russian, Kyrgyz, and English;
- document-retention, privacy, and customer-consent rules;
- refund, cancellation, complaint, and incident-handling procedures.

Do not infer these facts from old chats, demo data, marketing copy, or AI output.
