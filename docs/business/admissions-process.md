# EVO Admissions Operating Process

| Field | Value |

| Owner | EVO operations; named owner required |
| Status | Draft aligned with the implemented CRM model |
| Last verified | 2026-07-12 |
| Sources | CRM stages and contracts, company charter, EVO brand research |

This process separates a sales inquiry from the longer student-admissions file.
They are related, but they are not the same record.

## 1. Inquiry And Qualification

1. Capture the inquiry and communication consent.
2. Resolve or create the contact and lead in amoCRM.
3. Record target country, study level, intended intake, language level, budget,
   available documents, and the decision-making participants.
4. Schedule and complete a consultation.
5. Record the next action, owner, and deadline.

amoCRM owns canonical contact/lead identity and sales stage. EVO CRM and EVO
Inbox keep linked shadow identifiers for operator speed.

## 2. Contract And Student File

After a verified commercial agreement, create or link the student operational
file. Assign the responsible manager/curator and record the agreed service scope.
Do not treat a WhatsApp conversation alone as a signed client relationship.

## 3. Admissions Strategy And Documents

- confirm country, degree, program direction, intake, and realistic constraints;
- create a document checklist and track versions/status;
- record language tests or other entry requirements when evidenced;
- record owner and deadline for every missing item.

## 4. Applications And Decisions

Each university/program application is its own object with its own deadline and
status. Record submission evidence, provider response, offer/rejection, and next
action. Do not flatten multiple applications into one sales-stage field.

## 5. Visa, Finance, And Enrollment

Track visa work, payment obligations, enrollment evidence, accommodation or
adaptation work, and unresolved risks separately. A visa or admission outcome is
owned by the external authority, not guaranteed by EVO or the software.

## 6. Completion And Handover

Confirm what was delivered, what remains open, where final documents are stored,
and who owns future support. Close only when the operational record has evidence,
not merely because the sales lead moved stage.
