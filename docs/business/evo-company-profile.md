## Stated Service Scope

The charter allows EVO Admissions to provide:

- consultation for applicants planning study outside the Kyrgyz Republic;
- help preparing, collecting, and formatting admissions documents;
- support submitting applications to foreign education institutions and
  receiving application results;
- visa-process support and visa-related consultation;
- migration, accommodation, and adaptation assistance related to study abroad.

This is legal scope, not proof that every service is currently sold or staffed.
The active commercial offer must be maintained separately by an approved owner.
