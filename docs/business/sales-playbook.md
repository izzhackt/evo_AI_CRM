## Objective

The first conversation should understand the applicant, explain the process
truthfully, and agree one next action. It should not promise admission, a grant,
a visa, a deadline, a price, or a provider result that has not been verified.

## First Consultation Flow

1. Introduce yourself and confirm whether it is convenient to discuss the
   applicant's plans.
2. Ask who the applicant is and who participates in the decision.
3. Clarify current education, target country, degree/program, and intake.
4. Clarify language level, exams, available documents, budget range, and known
   deadlines.
5. Summarize what you understood and correct any mismatch.
6. Explain only the relevant part of EVO's process: consultation, document
   preparation, application support, visa consultation, and related adaptation
   support where actually included in the approved offer.
7. Agree the next action, owner, date, and communication channel.
8. Record the facts and follow-up in amoCRM. Do not leave the decision only in a
   personal chat.

## Safe Responses To Common Questions

**Can you guarantee admission, scholarship, or visa approval?**

No. Universities, scholarship bodies, and government authorities make those
decisions. EVO can organize the process, improve document readiness, track
requirements, and make risks visible.

**How much does it cost?**

Use only the current owner-approved service/package matrix. No authoritative
price matrix is stored in this repository yet, so the current safe action is to
escalate to the responsible commercial owner.

**Which university is best for me?**

First confirm academic profile, goals, budget, language, intake, and constraints.
Do not present a partner relationship or generic ranking as proof of fit.

**Can AI answer the applicant automatically?**

AI may prepare a draft from approved knowledge. A human operator reviews it.
Automatic replies are outside the approved first-launch boundary.

## Escalate Instead Of Guessing

Escalate pricing exceptions, legal/visa conclusions, complaint/refund decisions,
data-deletion requests, provider failures, unsupported languages, and any claim
that could materially affect an applicant's decision.
