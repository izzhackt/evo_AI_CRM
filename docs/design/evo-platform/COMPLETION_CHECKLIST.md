# EVO Platform frontend completion checklist

Completion may be reported only when every applicable item below exists in the
real application, is connected to the reviewed prototype, and has browser
evidence. A static example in the design-system catalogue does not by itself
complete an application state.

## Product foundation

- [ ] Source hierarchy and assumptions documented.
- [ ] Information architecture complete.
- [ ] Roles and permissions matrix complete.
- [ ] Sales stages and operational student stages are visibly distinct.
- [ ] amoCRM ownership and sync/conflict states are represented.
- [ ] AI customer communication is draft-only.

## Design system

- [ ] Brand-compliant foundations and logo treatment.
- [ ] Color, typography, spacing, grid, radius, border, elevation and motion.
- [ ] Navigation, forms, tables, kanban, status, modal, drawer and feedback.
- [ ] Inbox/chat, document, timeline, notification and audit components.
- [ ] Hover, focus, selected, disabled, read-only and responsive states.
