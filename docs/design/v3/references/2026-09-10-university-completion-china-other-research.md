# China and other-country university source review

Snapshot: 2026-09-10, final source inventory completed 2026-09-11.

## Result and boundaries

China publication candidate: **47 institutions, 94 selected programmes, including 27 language programmes**. This is a curated entry catalogue with official full-catalogue links, not a claim to reproduce every degree offered by every university. The runtime dataset is `src/lib/server/university-catalog-reviewed-china.json`; the machine-readable discovery registry is `2026-09-10-university-source-roster.json`.

No source originals or knowledge-base content were modified. Only institution-specific business materials were read. Trash, secrets, private applicant records and correspondence were excluded. Raw paths, access links and applicant information are not published here. Archive filenames and hashes below identify safe institutional brochures only. Inclusion does not claim an EVO partnership, direct application agreement, visa guarantee, scholarship award or admission availability.

## Normalization and rejected assumptions

- BIT: one institution, two explicitly labelled programme campuses. Artificial Intelligence is Zhuhai; Aeronautical and Astronautical Engineering is Beijing. Existing BIT photo key is retained and its caption must identify Zhuhai.
- HIT Shenzhen brochure remains provenance for the same HIT institution. Its May 2025 deadline is not reused for the main campus or a new intake.
- Huashang Vocational College is kept under its actual institutional name, not renamed to Huashang College or represented as a research university.
- Shanghai Normal / Shanghai University / Shanghai International Studies / University of Shanghai for Science and Technology are separate institutions. ECNU and ECUST are also distinct.
- Chinese-language courses use `language`, not `foundation`, unless an official university actually calls its programme a foundation/preparatory course.
- SWUFE in Chengdu is distinct from Southwest University in Chongqing.
- China University of Petroleum (East China) is distinct from its Beijing namesake. CUZ is distinct from Communication University of China in Beijing.
- Unnamed Nanning 2+3, unnamed Xiamen secondary vocational and unnamed Shanghai 1+3 offerings are excluded. Guangxi Overseas Chinese School and the named Shanghai school are not promoted to universities.
- The missing `Программы.xlsx` reference is an explicit coverage gap, not evidence that the linked workbook was inspected.
- Some historical Notion Italian city fields are wrong (for example Ca' Foscari recorded as Padua). Canonical city fixes require official sources; the original note is not silently treated as authoritative.
- Recognition directories, transfer partners and corporate logos do not create additional EVO-direct university records.

## Current information and limitations

All candidate China cards carry official institution and programme source links. Unknown values remain null or `needs_reconfirmation`. Specific 2026 deadlines already passed are `closed`, with no invented 2027 replacement. Only explicitly announced future rounds are marked announced, not automatically open.

The language-course offerings for Huashang Vocational College, Hubei University of Arts and Science and China Jiliang University have institutional evidence plus historical business material, but current course-specific detail was not fully retrievable. Their notes explicitly require confirmation. Official attachment challenges were not bypassed. Legacy USST/XISU/CUZ/ZISU course descriptions were not silently converted into new guaranteed offers. UESTC's historical English bachelor brochure is labelled as requiring reconfirmation; the separately published 2026 graduate programme information is treated independently.
