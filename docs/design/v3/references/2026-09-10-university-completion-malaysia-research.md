# Malaysia university catalogue research — 2026-09-10

## Decision and coverage

Prepared **13 institution cards / 39 selected programmes** for the reviewed catalogue. Nine retain the existing canonical names and city values; four additions are MSU, UniKL, UPM and UTM. Institution coverage is complete for the eligible Malaysia institutional source families identified in this run. Programme coverage is deliberately **curated, not exhaustive**: every card links to the current official full catalogue or international programme list.

This report contains public institutional facts and source decisions only. Original files remain unchanged; no applicant records, credentials, banking details, private correspondence, raw text exports or private absolute paths are included. Presence in EVO source materials does not establish an active commercial partnership.

## Important corrections and unresolved fields

- **City qualification levels:** the archived BACHELOR PROGRAMMES workbook contains diploma entries. Current qualification levels follow the [official business programme table](https://city.edu.my/business-management/), not the file title. Legacy FULL SCHOLARSHIP statements are not approved as current offers.
- **APU dates:** [Computer Science](https://www.apu.edu.my/course/bsc-hons-in-computer-science) publishes 28 September and 24 November 2026 starts. Foundation's official search listing and programme page have differed on the November day; that record retains only the month with a reconfirmation warning. A start date is never converted into an application deadline.
- **Sunway calendars:** the business analytics bachelor lists February/April/September, while Computer Science lists January/April/September. The [MSc Computer Science page](https://sunwayuniversity.edu.my/faculty-of-engineering-and-technology/courses/master-of-science-computer-science) currently says rolling intake, superseding the fixed intake pattern in older brochures.
- **Campus separation:** INTI Nilai is distinct from Subang/Penang/Sabah colleges. Foundation courses in legacy Sunway/Taylor material may belong to their colleges. UCSI Foundation in Arts in this selection is explicitly **Bandar Springhill**, while business bachelor/MBA are Kuala Lumpur.
- **Monash:** the three-year Computer Science degree is not the separate additional Honours year. The card is Malaysia-specific; it does not promise admission or transfer to Australia.
- **MSU:** programme pages label displayed fees as local/Malaysian. They must not become international tuition claims. [International admissions](https://www.msu.edu.my/international-students/admissions) asks for applications at least two months before the intended intake; a precise future date remains unknown.
- **UniKL:** current Software Engineering and Cybersecurity titles differ from legacy URL slugs. MIIT programme pages contain unrelated communications and BMI contact template blocks; those are excluded. AI's page states provisional accreditation; no full-accreditation claim is made. Registration dates in the 2026 programme PDF are not application deadlines.
- **UPM:** faculty and central pages differ in their description of the autumn intake. A linked central registration/fee guide is dated 2025. No unverified 2026 fee, future deadline or exact start date is asserted. Programme names are confirmed by [FSKTM](https://fsktm.upm.edu.my/?L=en); precise duration and individual eligibility remain null/pending.
- **UTM:** [international offerings](https://admission.utm.my/offered-allcourses-international/) distinguish programmes open to international applicants by semester. For September 2026, [the application page](https://admission.utm.my/apply-now/) gives a final undergraduate phase ending **17 July 2026**; the catalogue records this as closed. It is not the postgraduate deadline. Closed 2026/27 management/accounting/digital-marketing/manufacturing offerings are not advertised as open.
- **XMUM professional status:** the Computer Science and Technology page cautions that the degree is not an engineering discipline recognised by Board of Engineers Malaysia. Its English “Engineering” title is not treated as professional engineer registration.
- **Prices and scholarships:** 2025 raw tables, generic currency conversions, automatic scholarships and universal IELTS/GPA claims are not current offers. Links to official international fee schedules are retained where available. Fees can differ by citizenship, campus, annual versus total basis, SST and additional charges; obtain the applicable university quotation before a commercial commitment.
