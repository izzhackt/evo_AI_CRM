# Turkey university completion — reviewed evidence

Reviewed: 10–11 September 2026. Scope: all 11 Turkish institutions in the institutional university-note roster, with a curated selection of 22 actual bachelor programmes. This is institution coverage, not an exhaustive inventory of every degree each university offers.

## Source inventory and treatment

The 11 eligible institutional notes were read from the internal Notion-derived university materials and matched by institution identity to the source roster. They describe Ankara, Bahçeşehir, Bilkent, Boğaziçi, Istanbul, Koç, METU/ODTÜ, Sabancı, Yeditepe, İstanbul Aydın and İTÜ. No applicant record, correspondence, trash, credentials or private contact data was used. Original files were not modified. Public-safe canonical keys remain in the existing roster; this report does not reproduce raw exports.

Legacy institution profiles were used to find the universities, not to certify current admissions conditions. Current official university pages take priority for programme names, qualification level, language, campus and study format. Prices, university-wide examination thresholds, dated rankings, blanket scholarship promises and unverified seasonal deadlines are excluded.

## Material distinctions and unresolved fields

- **Ankara:** the [2026 engineering department list](https://www.eng.ankara.edu.tr/bolumler/) separates Turkish and English Computer Engineering. Biomedical Engineering is still listed; its [official 2025 brochure](https://bme.eng.ankara.edu.tr/wp-content/uploads/sites/47/2025/07/BME_brosur_2025_ENG.pdf) supports English and the four-year curriculum. The [new Ankara–SUNY route](https://bme.eng.ankara.edu.tr/prospective-students/) is a separate two-country programme, not substituted for the ordinary programme. No US work-permission or scholarship claims from that page are repeated. Confirm the applicable international intake before recommendation.
- **BAU:** Business Administration's official international page specifies Beşiktaş South. Old accreditation periods on the Software Engineering page are not asserted as current.
- **Bilkent:** the current Management catalogue was regenerated on 9 September 2026. The [international admission page](https://w3.bilkent.edu.tr/international/how-to-apply/) distinguishes minimum application conditions from selection; reaching a minimum does not guarantee a place.
- **Boğaziçi:** the Computer Engineering plan is explicitly Curriculum 2025. Management's [A/B curriculum groups](https://mgmt.bogazici.edu.tr/undergraduate/curriculum-0) are administrative cohort allocations, not separate specialisations. No unsupported duration was added to Management.
- **Istanbul University:** the [faculty's English programme list](https://international-iktisat.istanbul.edu.tr/en/content/general-information/welcome) distinguishes fully English Economics/MIS from Business Administration with 30% English. Conflicting totals in the page introduction are not used. The university-wide [EBS catalogue](https://ebs.istanbul.edu.tr/) is linked for other programmes.
- **Koç:** the international CASE page supports the four-year English Business Administration degree. The separate domestic/internal-transfer application record is not used as a first-year international admission deadline. Some official pages returned an access error to direct fetch but their official indexed content was readable; no authenticated area was bypassed.
- **METU:** this entry is the Ankara institution. The Northern Cyprus campus and METU–SUNY joint programmes are not merged into these degrees. The [2026/27 international catalogue](https://iso.metu.edu.tr/tr/node/61) contains quotas; a quota is not proof that applications are open or places remain.
- **Sabancı:** the [official bachelor list](https://iro.sabanciuniv.edu/en/all-bachelors-programs) specifies four-year, English programmes. Required language preparation can add time and is not included as a guaranteed zero-cost component.
- **Yeditepe:** standard Business Administration is separate from the UNCW joint route. The current prospective-student fee table contains unclear/inconsistent amounts and old programme pages contain historic fee/accreditation years, so no monetary amount or current accreditation promise is imported.
- **İstanbul Aydın:** the [international list](https://study.aydin.edu.tr/programs/) separates Undergraduate from Associate. Associate Business Management must not be presented as Bachelor Business Administration. Programme names/languages are confirmed; unverified durations remain null.
- **İTÜ:** Computer Engineering has 30% and 100% English options; this card explicitly selects 100%. The 2021–2024 archived curriculum is not relabelled as the new student's current plan. AI and Data Engineering's official qualification page confirms four-year English study; old exam thresholds are not advertised as current.

## Publication limits

Absence of an intake in this reviewed selection means “confirm with the university”, not “the programme does not admit students”. Fees and scholarships require the applicable current university quotation/award.
