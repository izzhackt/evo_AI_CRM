# Additional destinations — university completion evidence

Reviewed: 11 September 2026. Scope: eight Cyprus institutions plus University of Vienna, GBS Dubai, Tongmyong University and Schiller International University. Candidate output contains 12 institutions and 18 curated programmes; it is not each university's complete course catalogue.

## Institutional source coverage

The ten eligible institutional notes for Cyprus, Vienna and GBS were read. Tongmyong's approved internal institutional derivative was read and matched against its current International College pages. Schiller's 227-page institutional catalogue was identified in the raw business-material archive; its campus disclosures and selected programme sections were inspected. It is a 2024/25 catalogue, not a current admission offer.

No personal applicant file, private correspondence, trash, secret or raw credential material was used. Raw originals were preserved. Public-safe programme facts were paraphrased and verified with official university/provider sources; source location is not treated as evidence of campus identity.

## Corrections that affect student choices

- **Cyprus University of Technology:** the Computer Engineering and Informatics page explicitly says four years and describes computer systems, databases, AI and a final-year thesis. It does not explicitly confirm English as the programme's teaching language. The URL's English-language setting and presence of academic-English modules are not evidence that the degree is English-taught, so language remains null.
- **Eastern Mediterranean University:** the [department page](https://www.emu.edu.tr/en/academics/faculties/faculty-of-engineering/computer-engineering/748) distinguishes English Computer Engineering, English Software Engineering and Turkish Computer Engineering. The institution is in Famagusta in the northern part of Cyprus. The card does not make Republic of Cyprus, EU access, visa or automatic recognition promises.
- **European University Cyprus:** the Computer Science page confirms Nicosia, English, four years, 240 ECTS and an October 2026 start. This becomes an announced month, not a claimed open admission window or inferred deadline. Frankfurt programmes are not conflated with Nicosia.
- **Frederick:** Computer Science and Computer Engineering specify English and Nicosia–Limassol. Four years refers to full-time study. Both pages describe mathematics/English placement checks; a foundation requirement depends on the individual result.
- **Near East University:** the last successfully readable official programme/language/year listing was [2025/26](https://aday.neu.edu.tr/2025-2026-academic-year-program-fees/?lang=en). It supports the identity of four-year English Computer Engineering, not a verified open 2026/27 intake or current fee. The [current faculty site](https://aif.neu.edu.tr/) remains available but contains old course-calendar labels. The next intake is explicitly marked needs_reconfirmation. This institution is in the northern part of Nicosia, not silently merged with other Cyprus admission routes.
- **UCLan Cyprus:** the official title is BSc (Hons) Computing, not Computer Science. Its page confirms English, 240 ECTS and full-/part-time modes. January/September references are recurring-entry information, not a confirmed future deadline. Exact duration was not explicit in the selected current programme page and remains null. Campus location is Pyla in the Larnaca area.
- **University of Cyprus:** its official qualification entry says Greek, eight semesters and 240 ECTS for Computer Science. The programme catalogue index currently labels itself 2023/24; current plan and admissions details require a fresh check with the department. A blanket legacy IELTS requirement is not adequate for this Greek-taught bachelor.
- **University of Nicosia:** the bachelor page confirms English/four years/240 ECTS. The department lists both conventional and distance MSc options with Cyber Security, Mobile Systems and Blockchain Technologies concentrations. The MSc card does not assume language or equate an online offer with an on-campus visa route. Athens offerings and old medical joint-degree descriptions are not imported.
- **Vienna:** both selected bachelors are German-taught, six semesters and 180 ECTS. The [central bachelor list](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/degree-programmes/bachelordiploma-programmes/) requires German C1 to begin. Registration for their 2026/27 entrance process ended 4 May 2026; these records are closed, not advertised as future openings. The July examination dates are not used as application deadlines. The institution is University of Vienna, not the separate Medical University of Vienna.
- **GBS Dubai:** the current UAE brand/site is Global Business Studies, while the historical roster label says Global Banking School. The canonical roster name is retained, but the overview makes the current provider identity explicit. HND International in Business is a two-year **diploma awarded by Pearson**, not a university bachelor's degree. A later top-up or transfer requires a separate receiving-institution decision; neither admission nor a one-year top-up is guaranteed. January/March/June/September are published recurring intake months, without a confirmed new application window.
- **Tongmyong:** the current International College list uses BA Global Business and BS AI & Computer Engineering. This is the English-track International College in Busan, not all Korean-language departments. The [central page with a 2025 guide](https://www.tu.ac.kr/english/sub02_02.do) and [International College requirements](https://www.tu.ac.kr/ic/sub03_04.do) differ on TOEFL/TEPS thresholds, so no universal minimum or old agency deadline is published. Exact programme duration remains unconfirmed.
- **Schiller:** the raw file sits in a Dubai-labelled folder, but its own disclosures identify Tampa as the US main campus and Heidelberg, Madrid and Paris as branch campuses. The [current programme catalogue](https://www.schiller.edu/programs/) lists the same four cities, not Dubai. The card has country US with all real campus cities explicitly shown. Its 2024/25 catalogue and current pages differ on Computer Science campus/dual-degree availability; no guaranteed dual award, transfer, OPT or scholarship claim is made. Published 21 September 2026 and 1 February 2027 starts are announced, not application deadlines.
